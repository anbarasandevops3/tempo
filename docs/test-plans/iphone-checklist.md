## A. Reliability (the reason this app exists)
- [ ] A1. Start "Run / walk intervals", lock the phone and put it away. All cues arrive on time until "Workout complete".
- [ ] A2. During a workout, switch to another app for 2 minutes, then come back: the screen shows the right step and time.
- [ ] A3. Same as A1 with Bluetooth headphones/AirPods.
- [ ] A4. Ring/silent switch set to silent: cues still play.
- [ ] A5. Lock screen shows Tempo with play/pause and skip; each works.
- [ ] A6. A notification arrives mid-workout: cues continue.
- [ ] A7. Phone call (or Siri) mid-workout: after it ends, press play on the lock screen or in the app and it continues from where it paused.
- [ ] A8. Swipe Tempo away mid-workout, reopen: "Unfinished" banner, Resume continues from about the same point.

## B. Everyday use
- [ ] B1. Voice is clear over street noise at normal volume; beeps are distinct from the voice.
- [ ] B2. Countdown is readable at arm's length in sunlight.
- [ ] B3. Building a custom workout (steps, repeats, durations) is quick and the keyboard doesn't get in the way.
- [ ] B4. "Screen on" keeps the screen awake while the workout plays.
- [ ] B5. Added to the home screen: opens full screen, no Safari bars. (needs https hosting)
- [ ] B6. Airplane mode: the home-screen app still opens and runs a workout. (needs https hosting)
